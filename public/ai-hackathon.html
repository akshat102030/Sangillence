Why AI Synergy is different

This is not another "build an AI app" hackathon.

Most challenges tell you the problem and ask for a solution. Here, teams are expected to investigate an ambiguous real-world situation, decide what the actual problem is, use evidence to challenge assumptions, work with AI critically, and justify why their chosen solution deserves to exist.

The Journey: Case → Investigation → Problem Discovery → AI Collaboration → Decision → Solution → Build → Twist → Adapt → Demonstrate

More than a hackathon. An opportunity to build what comes next.

From investigation to implementation

Round 1 — Case Investigation & Problem Selection

Format: Online elimination round

Your team must show that it can understand the case, identify stakeholders, investigate evidence, formulate competing problems, challenge assumptions, use AI critically, select a meaningful problem, propose a feasible solution, and define what should be built next.

Deliverables: Case Investigation Brief • Solution Presentation • AI Conversation Logs • Decision Trail • Basic SRS • References • Team Contribution Declaration

Offline Round — Build, Adapt & Demonstrate

Venue: ABV-IIITM Gwalior

Shortlisted teams build a functional slice of the proposed solution. At a common announced point, organizers introduce new evidence or constraints. Teams must decide whether and how to adapt, then demonstrate and defend their final result live.

How teams are evaluated

Round 1 Evaluation — 100 Points

Criterion Weight What judges look for
Problem Diagnosis & Selection 25% Depth of case understanding, competing problem statements, justification
Evidence & Investigation Quality 20% Source quality, verification, assumptions, uncertainty handling
Human–AI Collaboration 25% Purposeful AI use for exploration, critique, verification and decisions
Solution / SRS Quality 20% Feasibility, workflow clarity, requirements, AI/human responsibilities
Context, Ethics & Accountability 10% Privacy, fairness, inclusion, risks, safeguards, oversight

Offline Round Evaluation — 100 Points

Criterion Weight What judges look for
Execution / Working Prototype 30% Whether the core workflow works and addresses the selected problem
Adaptation to New Constraint 20% Quality of reasoning and implementation after the twist
AI Orchestration / Synergy 20% Effective division of work between people, models and tools
Evaluation, Testing & Safeguards 20% Tests, failure cases, robustness, limitations and safety
Communication & Team Ownership 10% Clear explanation, ownership, technical understanding

Final ranking formula: 30% Round 1 + 70% Offline Round
